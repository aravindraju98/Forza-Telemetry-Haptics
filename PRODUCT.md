# PRODUCT.md

## What this is

A Windows desktop mixer that turns Forza Horizon 6 Data Out telemetry into XInput rumble. The user drives with one eye on the road and glances here to confirm the link, the pad, and which haptic layer is speaking.

## Users

Someone already in a race. They know the game. They need status and sliders, not explanation.

## Mode

Operate. Design serves the task. Read in a dim room, next to a game window.

## Voice

Calm, clinical, short. Instrument labels. No hype, no affiliation banner in the chrome.

## Anti-references

Purple-to-blue SaaS gradients. Nested rounded cards. Inter-everywhere marketing dashboards. “Beautifully crafted.” Pulsing live dots. Italic serif kickers.

## Constraints

WPF desktop, not a browser. Must stay usable at a glance while Forza has focus. All existing controls and live updates stay; only the surface changes.
